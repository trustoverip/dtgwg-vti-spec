[[def: notification]]

~ A signed statement by an originator, addressed to one identifier — an individual, a group, a community or a network — that a thing exists which the addressee, or the parties it stands for, may act on or take part in. It names the thing's holder, the thing's identifier and the operation that opens it, and the audience the thing concerns; it confers no authority and is no evidence of eligibility.

~ A party that stands for others may relay a notification to them, carrying the originator's signed document unchanged, so that the final recipient can verify who originated it.
