[[def: threshold custodian]]

~ A group of n members — each a recovery custodian in its own [[ref: protection boundary]], holding one whole recovery key of its own — of which any t (2 ≤ t < n) must authorize a recovery. A condition over whole keys, declared in the identifier document and counted by the witness and by verifiers, not a threshold signature scheme: nothing is shared or assembled, and each member checks and signs the exact entry with its own key.
