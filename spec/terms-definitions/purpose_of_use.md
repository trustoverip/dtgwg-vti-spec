[[def: purpose of use, purpose]]

~ What a subject may use content it reads for, stated on the [[ref: access control entry]] that grants the reading: `any`, or a list of purposes registered by this specification or named by IRI. A request for content names the purpose it is made for, and the node records it.

~ A declaration, not a proof: a node cannot observe use. It refuses a read made for a purpose the entry does not grant, and keeps the declared purpose of every read it serves.
