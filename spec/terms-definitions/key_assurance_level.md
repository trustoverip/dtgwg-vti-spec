[[def: key assurance level]]

~ How many of the layered key protections a [[ref: service DID]] has, chosen by its operator and declared in its identifier document: level 1, a single key; level 2, role-separated, with the `attestation` and `update` keys held by the VTA; level 3, recoverable, with those keys generated and in no backup, a recovery key held by a [[ref: recovery custodian]] outside the VTA, and a witness enforcing it. A verifier's policy decides which level it accepts for each use.

~ Not an [[ref: assurance level]], which is the strength of the authentication behind a session.
